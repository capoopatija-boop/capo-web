What does this service involve?

Ozone treatment may be used as a supplementary measure for some persistent vehicle odours, including smoke or pet smells. Removing the source remains essential.

How is the work approached?

The cabin must be empty of people and animals during treatment. Equipment, exposure conditions, ventilation and safe re-entry must follow the manufacturer’s procedures.

What should you know before visiting?

Ozone is a respiratory irritant. Treatment must not be performed in an occupied vehicle. It cannot guarantee permanent odour removal, particularly if moisture or contamination remains.

Useful care advice

For regular upkeep, deal with fresh dirt or spills promptly and choose a treatment suited to the material. Avoid harsh household chemicals that can affect vehicle surfaces. The actual method and outcome depend on inspection and on the condition of your vehicle. Please call to confirm details before arrival.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to arrange a time?

We recommend calling before your visit so we can discuss availability, the vehicle condition and the scope of work.

How long will it take?

The time required depends on the vehicle, its condition and the agreed treatment. Contact us for an individual estimate.

Where is CAPO located?

We are at Tošina 16 in Volosko, Opatija, at Tošina Shopping Centre. Use the map button for directions.

Will every stain or defect disappear?

No result can be guaranteed without assessing the vehicle. Some marks, damage and odours require further work or cannot be eliminated by this service.
